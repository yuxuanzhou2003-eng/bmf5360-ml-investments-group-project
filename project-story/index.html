BMF5360 · ML INVESTMENTS
Current research briefing

Momentum first.
ML must earn its place.

Our current AI-industry fund benchmark is simple 20-day momentum. Machine-learning models are not the chosen strategy yet: they are challengers that must beat this simple rule after costs and risk controls.

Plain-English takeawayThe simple strategy currently wins. That is useful: it gives us a hard, honest target for every ML idea.
Research status: development-stage evidence only. The 2024-2025 comparison period has been reused, so it is not a final out-of-sample win. The 2026 test period remains sealed until rules are frozen.
01 · Investment idea

Which AI subsectors should we hold - and how much?

The fund works in layers: decide the total AI exposure, choose stronger subsectors, then hold eligible companies within those subsectors. Cash is the defensive alternative.

02 · Current baseline

Simple 20-day momentum

Every five trading days, rank the AI subsectors by their recent 20-day return, select the top three, and hold their eligible companies. It uses a transparent rule, not a black box.

04 · ML 的角色

A challenger, not a winner by default

Logistic Regression gives an explainable benchmark. Random Forest can capture more complicated interactions. Both must be tested against exactly the same trading delay, costs, risk limits and momentum benchmark.

Earlier 49-stock Logistic results are retained as a separate stock-selection pilot. They are not the current fund’s main strategy.
05 · Data

AI industry, split into subsectors

The working universe contains AI-related US-listed companies grouped into subsectors. Membership history is not fully point-in-time, so the current result remains exploratory.

06 · Professional safeguards

No peeking at the answer

Features must be known before the trade. Training uses earlier dates; the final test stays sealed. We compare net performance, turnover, drawdown, costs and concentration - not just a flattering ML score.

07 · 下一步

How ML can earn a place

  1. Freeze the exact rules.
  2. Test once on sealed 2026 data.
  3. Adopt ML only if it beats momentum after costs and risk controls.
